Privacy Policy

Applies to DayNote, MindGym, Wishing You, Simple Quran App, Numberlight and Revisely (“the apps”) by Muhammad Burhan. Last updated: 16 November 2026.

Your content

Notes, events, tasks, moods, game progress, revision progress, imported files (such as trial balances in Numberlight) and other things you create are stored and processed on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, they are stored in your own private iCloud account, which is controlled by Apple. We do not collect, see or sell your content.

Advertising

The free versions of the apps show ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device’s advertising identifier, approximate location (from your IP address), app usage and diagnostic data. In the UK and EU you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time from the app’s Settings. You can read how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your iPhone’s Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.

Calendar, notifications and Face ID

If you allow it, DayNote reads your Apple Calendar events only to show them in the app; nothing is changed or sent anywhere. Reminders are scheduled on your device. Face ID / passcode unlocking is handled by Apple, and we never receive your biometric data.

Location

If you allow it, Simple Quran App uses your location only to work out accurate prayer times and the Qibla direction. This is done on your device and your location is not sent to us. You can turn location access off at any time in your iPhone’s Settings.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
