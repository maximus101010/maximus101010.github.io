Restore a subscription

Open the app, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

Cancel a subscription

On your iPhone, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions, choose the app and tap Cancel.

Notes not syncing?

Check you are signed into iCloud on both devices and that iCloud sync is turned on in the app’s Settings.
